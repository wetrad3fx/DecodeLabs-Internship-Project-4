Dataset

The dataset contains sales transaction information, including fields such as:

- Order ID
- Date
- Product
- Quantity
- Total Price
- Order Status
- Payment Method
- Referral Source
- Coupon Code

The dataset contains 1,200 orders.



Analysis Performed

1. Product Performance


- Number of orders per product
- Total quantity sold
- Total revenue
- Average order value

This helped identify the products generating the highest revenue.

2. Order Status Analysis

Orders were analyzed according to their status, including:

- Delivered
- Shipped
- Pending
- Returned
- Cancelled

This provided an overview of the current state of customer orders.

3. Referral Source Analysis

Revenue and order volume were analyzed by referral source to determine which marketing channels generated the most business.

4. Payment Method Analysis

The dataset was analyzed based on payment methods to determine which methods contributed the most orders and revenue.

5. Yearly & Monthly Revenue Analysis

Revenue was analyzed over time to identify:

- Yearly performance
- Monthly revenue trends
- High-performing periods


Visualizations

Several visualizations were created to communicate the findings effectively:

Revenue by Product — Bar Chart
Revenue by Year — Column Chart
Monthly Revenue Trend — Line Chart
Orders by Status — Pie Chart
Revenue by Referral Source — Column Chart

These visualizations make it easier to understand patterns and compare different categories.


Key Insights

Some of the major findings from the analysis include:

Total Revenue:$1,264,761.96
Total Orders:1,200
Average Order Value: $1,053.97
Top Product by Revenue: Chair
Top Referral Source: Instagram
Highest Revenue Year:2023
Most Common Order Status: Cancelled
Highest-Revenue Payment Method: Credit Card
Top Coupon by Revenue: FREESHIP

These findings provide useful information about product performance, customer behavior, marketing channels, and overall sales performance.
